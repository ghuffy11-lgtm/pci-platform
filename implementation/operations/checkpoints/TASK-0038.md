### What this task must produce, restated from the union of both rulings

**The question:** can a **kernel-constrained / in-query authorization path**, and/or an architecture
that **prevents security-relevant projection divergence**, obtain **E1–E4 and G-Q4 evidence without
examining unauthorized content**? MSG-0115 identified this alternative and **explicitly did not measure
it**; both rulings forbid assuming it works.

**The operative addition from MSG-0116b** — and the reason a runner reading only MSG-0116a would build
the wrong instrument: **the re-check must be instrumented *separately* from retrieval-content
examination**, with evidence that it reads **only authoritative kernel facts**; and **a purported
re-check that reads content-bearing data from an unauthorized candidate is examination and fails
Shape-1.**

**The operative addition from MSG-0116a:** **the measured kernel-read count is not by itself a Shape-1
violation.** Taken together these two fix the instrument design: the re-check's reads are counted on a
**separate** counter from `U`, and that counter is **classified** into metadata reads and
content-bearing reads, with any content-bearing read from an unauthorized candidate reported as a
Shape-1 failure rather than as a number.

**Nothing may be cleared by Q8.** E1–E4 and G-Q4/G-Q5/G-Q6/G-Q7 remain independently necessary.
