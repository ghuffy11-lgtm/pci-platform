**The row was rewritten correctly, committed and pushed as a reconciliation — it was neither trusted
as found nor discarded.**

- **Trusting it as found was refused.** An uncommitted, unattributable, encoding-corrupted edit to the

  TASK-0046 is authorized and is the work to do — is **independently established by two committed Lead

  TASK-0045"*) is the row that made **TASK-0045 READY**, and it carries the Claude co-author trailer.
  **Queue reconciliation is transcription of a committed Lead authorization, not self-authorization**,
  and the row written here traces to MSG-0157 and to the Lead's own task file rather than to the
