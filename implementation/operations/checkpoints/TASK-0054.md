- **(a) accumulating across statements and readable back**, AND
- **(b) available in a form whose statement text does not carry inlined content?**

**The standing record answers each half on a different subject and never both together** — which is
precisely why the general question is worth asking:

| `node:sqlite` `sourceSQL` | **NO** — per-handle, no accumulation | **YES** | MSG-0168 §5.3 |
