### Two defects in the probe's own apparatus, found before any result was reported

**Recorded because a later reader needs them more than a tidy account**, and because TASK-0037 and
TASK-0038 both set that precedent.

| # | Defect | How it surfaced | Consequence had it stood |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The index-entry column was mislabelled.** The instrument receives `open_ended` and **cannot classify an entry**, so it counts entries **visited**, not entries **unauthorized**. The first draft printed the raw count under the heading `U1lb` | hand-checking the arithmetic against the cohort table: on the uniform fixture the two differ by a constant and the column *looked* right | **Overstatement of the unauthorized count** — the dangerous direction for a bound whose entire purpose is to prove failure. Fixed by splitting engine-measured **`Nidx`** from a **derived**, deliberately generous `U1lb` |
| 2 | **The probe asserted something its own output contradicted in the same line** — that the authorizer fires only at prepare time, "confirmed" by a printed execution-phase count of **101** | reading the probe's own output instead of trusting the sentence next to it | A **false characterisation of an instrument** the E1 argument rests on. Fixed by replacing the assertion with an invariance-with-`N` measurement (101 at M=500 **and** at M=5000 ⇒ a compilation event, not a counter) |

**And one trap the probe was built to avoid, worth recording because it is nearly invisible.** SQLite
**silently ignores an unrecognised pragma**, so `PRAGMA vdbe_trace = on` returns without error whether
or not tracing exists. The probe therefore runs **a pragma that certainly does not exist as a
control**, and every tracing pragma behaved identically to it. **Without that control this probe could
have reported E4 obtained from an instrument that was never running.**
