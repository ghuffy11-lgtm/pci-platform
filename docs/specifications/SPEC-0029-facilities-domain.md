# SPEC-0029 — Facilities Domain

**Status:** Domain specification
**Version:** 0.1

## Purpose

Represent physical facilities and connect buildings, rooms, utilities, equipment, maintenance, people, and incidents to the organizational model.

## Core Objects

Campus, building, floor, room, facility asset, utility, maintenance contract, work order, inspection, safety system, access point, and location.

## Capabilities

- Facility inventory.
- Location hierarchy.
- Asset and room relationships.
- Maintenance scheduling.
- Work-order integration.
- Incident correlation.
- Utility and environmental observations.
- Cross-domain impact analysis.

## Acceptance Criteria

A facility asset can be located precisely, associated with responsible teams and contracts, related to operational technology and helpdesk records, and used in authorized workflows.
