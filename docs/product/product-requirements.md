# PCI Product Requirements — Foundation

**Status:** Baseline
**Version:** 0.1

## Product Goal

PCI provides an on-premises, customer-controlled intelligence platform that connects organizational knowledge, operational systems, AI reasoning, and governed automation.

## Primary Outcomes

1. Understand the customer's organization through a connected Reality Model.
2. Provide secure natural-language access to authorized enterprise knowledge.
3. Assist operators with diagnosis, analysis, and decision support.
4. Execute approved operational actions through governed agents.
5. Preserve provenance and auditability.
6. Remain independent of any single AI model or vendor.
7. Support disconnected/private deployments.
8. Allow domain capabilities to be added without changing the core platform.

## Initial Use Cases

- Local enterprise AI and internal knowledge.
- Daily infrastructure monitoring and operational assistance.
- Network device discovery and governed configuration.
- Helpdesk integration.
- Facilities and biomedical operational integration.

## Non-Goals for Foundation

- Training a proprietary foundation model.
- Building a custom identity provider.
- Building a custom observability backend.
- Replacing every enterprise application.
- Fully autonomous production infrastructure changes.

## Product Quality Bar

Security, correctness, explainability, auditability, portability, and operational recoverability take precedence over feature count.
