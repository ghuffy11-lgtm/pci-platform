# PCI Product Vision

**ID:** PROD-VISION-0001  
**Status:** Founding  
**Authority:** Product direction

## Vision

PCI is a private, modular Enterprise Intelligence Platform that models an organization as connected reality so humans, AI models, and automation can reason over the same governed context.

## North Star

> **Model reality. Everything else follows.**

## What PCI Is

PCI is a platform for representing organizational reality, connecting operational systems, providing governed intelligence, and executing approved work through agents and automation.

## What PCI Is Not

- Not a single AI model.
- Not a chatbot product.
- Not a document repository.
- Not a CMDB replacement by default.
- Not a proprietary identity system when a standard can be adopted.
- Not dependent on one vendor, model, runtime, database, or deployment topology.

## Strategic Outcomes

1. Give an organization one coherent model of its people, assets, locations, applications, services, knowledge, policies, events, and relationships.
2. Allow humans and AI to consume the same governed context.
3. Automate approved operational work while preserving authorization and auditability.
4. Keep customer data inside the customer's environment.
5. Remain operational without mandatory Internet dependency after installation.
6. Allow AI models and infrastructure components to be replaced as technology evolves.

## Customer Zero

The first real deployment target is the founder's organization. Existing IT, Facilities, Biomedical, Helpdesk, network, infrastructure, and enterprise application use cases will be treated as real-world validation domains.

## Long-Term Direction

PCI may evolve into an organizational digital twin / knowledge operating system in which domain applications are views and capabilities over a shared reality model.

This is a strategic direction, not a requirement to implement the complete vision in version 1.
