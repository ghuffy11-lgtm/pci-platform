# PCI Provenance Model

## Purpose

Preserve where a fact came from, when it was observed, how it was transformed, and who or what accepted it.

## Provenance Elements

A provenance record may include:

- source identifier;
- source type;
- source location/reference;
- observed time;
- ingestion time;
- actor or connector;
- transformation/normalization step;
- confidence;
- validation status;
- related evidence.

## Authority

Not all sources have equal authority. PCI distinguishes source evidence from accepted organizational truth. A connector observation may be current evidence while an approved policy may be authoritative for governance.

## AI-Generated Knowledge

AI-generated statements are never authoritative solely because a model produced them. They must identify their source context and validation state.

## Retention

Provenance must remain available for governed facts and decisions for as long as the corresponding object or policy requires historical traceability.
