# PCI Knowledge Quality Standard

**ID:** GOV-KQ-0001  
**Status:** Founding

PCI must distinguish authoritative knowledge from assumptions and AI-generated suggestions.

## Quality Dimensions

- **Authority:** Is the source authoritative for this fact?
- **Provenance:** Can origin and transformation be traced?
- **Freshness:** When was it last verified?
- **Completeness:** Are required fields and relationships present?
- **Accuracy:** Has the information been validated?
- **Confidence:** How strong is the evidence?
- **Ownership:** Is a responsible owner identified?
- **Consistency:** Does it conflict with other authoritative knowledge?

## Suggested Confidence Levels

```text
UNKNOWN
LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
VERIFIED
```

Confidence is not authorization. A high-confidence fact does not grant permission to act.

## AI-Generated Knowledge

AI-generated information must be marked as derived/proposed until validated. AI may assist validation but must not self-authorize its own output as authoritative.
