# PCI Lexicon

**ID:** GOV-LEX-0001  
**Status:** Founding

## Core Terms

**Reality Model** — canonical semantic representation of entities, relationships, events, policies, provenance, and state.

**Knowledge Object** — a canonical typed entity or concept represented in the Reality Model.

**Knowledge Fabric** — platform capability that stores, validates, relates, queries, versions, and presents governed knowledge.

**Domain** — a coherent business or technical area owning a set of capabilities and knowledge concepts.

**Capability** — a stable business or technical outcome exposed by the platform, independent of its implementation.

**Agent** — an identity-bearing software actor capable of reasoning and/or executing approved tools.

**Tool** — an explicitly registered operation available to an agent.

**Provenance** — information describing the origin, derivation, or transformation of knowledge.

**View** — a human or machine presentation of the same canonical knowledge.

**Authoritative Source** — system or artifact designated as the authority for a particular fact or domain.

**Customer Zero** — the first real organizational deployment used to validate PCI in production-like conditions.

**Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** — durable record of an important architectural choice and its rationale.

**Specification (SPEC)** — testable definition of requirements and behavior for an implementation.

**Request for Comments (RFC)** — structured proposal used when a significant change needs review before becoming an approved decision or specification.

## Naming Rule

These terms have specific meanings inside PCI. New terminology should be added here rather than creating competing synonyms.
