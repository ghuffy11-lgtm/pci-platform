# PCI Product Vision

**ID:** PROD-VISION-0001  
**Status:** Founding Vision  
**Working Name:** PCI

## Mission

PCI is an Enterprise Intelligence Platform that helps organizations connect people, knowledge, systems, automation, and AI while keeping their data private, controlled, and usable.

## Vision

PCI will become a platform through which an organization can model its people, assets, locations, systems, processes, policies, knowledge, events, and relationships so humans and intelligent agents can reason about the organization and act safely within approved boundaries.

## Strategic Idea

**Model reality. Everything else follows.**

AI is an enabling capability, not the definition of the product. AI models, inference runtimes, vector stores, identity systems, and other infrastructure are replaceable components behind stable platform capabilities.

## Customer Value

PCI should help an organization:

- understand its operational reality;
- connect existing systems instead of replacing them unnecessarily;
- use private/local AI against approved organizational knowledge;
- automate repeatable work with controls and approvals;
- detect relationships and dependencies that are difficult to see across separate systems;
- preserve customer ownership of data and deployment;
- operate in disconnected or restricted environments when required.

## Customer Zero

The first serious validation environment is the founder's organization. Existing operational systems—including the organization's IT/Facilities/Biomedical helpdesk—are candidates for integration, not automatic replacement.

## Long-Term Direction

The platform may eventually support domains such as:

- IT and network operations
- Helpdesk and service management
- Facilities
- Biomedical engineering
- Corporate knowledge
- HR
- Finance
- Manufacturing
- Executive intelligence

These are domain capabilities built on a common platform model, not isolated products with duplicated foundations.
